Quick setup guide
  1. Create or join your company. The first owner uses the private setup token from Railway. Everyone else joins with an invitation from their company administrator.
  2. Choose roles and tools. Owners and admins assign access and job classifications. Developers receive the approved browsing tools; analyst and marketing classifications also receive Google Ads by default. Administrators can change these assignments.
  3. Sign in from TerminAI. Open the Team icon and use this manager’s URL and your account. Assigned tools are configured on that device. Google Ads requires your own Google authorization and any required developer token.
  4. Browse and edit. TerminAI installs its embedded browser MCP for Codex. Restart Codex inside a terminal tab, then ask it to use terminai_browser. Use the Browser icon for profiles, tabs and groups, or Files to edit a project.
  5. Share deliberately. Create a team conversation and choose who can see it. Enable “Share visible output” to publish the selected terminal’s output while Team is open. Teammates can reply; incoming messages are never run automatically as commands.

Your local terminal sessions remain available without a company login. Company administrators cannot read conversations marked private to their owner.
