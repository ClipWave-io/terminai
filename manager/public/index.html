How team workspaces work
  1. Join with an invitation. Your company owner sends you a private invitation link. Team members never need the server setup token.
  2. Connect your desktop. Open Team in TerminAI, enter this site’s URL, and sign in with your team account.
  3. Bring your agents. Sign in to Claude Code or Codex on your Mac. The desktop runs agents; this website manages access and shared conversations.
  4. Share deliberately. Choose a conversation and enable “Share visible output” only when you want to share. Incoming messages are never executed as commands.

Local terminal sessions work without a team account. Company admins cannot read conversations marked private to their owner.
