LocalFlow: talk instead of typing.

Hold fn, speak, let go: your words appear wherever your cursor is, in TerminAI, Claude Code or any app. Like Wispr Flow, but it runs on your Mac, works offline and is completely free.

Download for MacApple Silicon · signed · free Also get TerminAI, the terminal for Claude Code and Codex
Transcription runs on your Mac: nothing you say leaves it.

Set up in a minute

Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later.

  1. Download and open the file, then drag LocalFlow into Applications.
  2. Open LocalFlow. The first time it sets itself up: it downloads the speech model (about 2.5 GB, once).
  3. Allow the microphone and Accessibility when macOS asks.
  4. Hold fn and talk. Double-tap fn for hands-free. Settings are in the menu bar icon.
If fn opens Apple's dictation instead, log out and back in once.
