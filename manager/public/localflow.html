LocalFlow

Talk instead of typing. Hold fn, speak, let go: your words appear wherever your cursor is, in TerminAI, Claude Code or any app. Like Wispr Flow, but it runs on your Mac, works offline and is completely free.

  • On your Mac: transcription on Apple Silicon, nothing leaves the computer
  • 25+ languages, fast enough that the text is there as you let go
  • Hands-free: double-tap fn to keep talking

Install

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 13 and about 3 GB for the speech model. In a terminal (TerminAI works):

git clone https://github.com/livioai/localflow.git
cd localflow && ./install.sh

The installer adds LocalFlow to Applications and downloads the model once. Open it, allow the microphone and Accessibility, then hold fn and talk.
